1. Overview

WhatsApp message templates are pre-approved message formats that allow you to contact customers outside the 24-hour messaging window — for example for broadcast campaigns, appointment reminders, or order updates.

All templates must be reviewed and approved by Meta before use. This dashboard lets you create, manage, and monitor that entire process without leaving the app.

When do you need a template? Any time you start a conversation with a customer who hasn't messaged you in the last 24 hours — for example, a broadcast campaign to your full contact list.
Message TypeWhen it worksNeeds Meta Approval?
Free-form textWithin 24 hrs of customer's last messageNo
TemplateAny time — even after 24 hrsYes

2. Accessing the Templates Tab

Click the 📋 Templates tab in the top navigation bar (between Broadcasts and Settings).

The tab contains three sections stacked top to bottom:

  • Approval Notifications — banners shown when Meta approves or rejects a template
  • 🖼️ Assets Library — upload and manage images/videos for use in template headers
  • ➕ Create New Template — build and submit a template for Meta approval
  • 📋 Your Templates — view, filter, and delete existing templates

3. Assets Library — Uploading Images

Before creating a template with an image, video, or document header, upload the file here to get a permanent public URL.

1

Select your business

Use the Business dropdown at the top of the Assets Library card. Assets are stored per business.

2

Upload your file

Drag and drop the file onto the upload zone, or click to browse. Supported formats:

  • Images: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP
  • Video: MP4
  • Documents: PDF

Maximum file size: 10 MB.

3

Copy the public URL

After upload, the image appears in the gallery. Click 📋 Copy URL to copy the public link. This URL is what you use in template headers and broadcasts.

Tip: Uploaded assets are stored in Supabase Storage and served via a CDN. The URLs are permanent and publicly accessible — exactly what Meta requires for template headers and broadcast media.
Best image dimensions for WhatsApp template headers: 800 × 418 px (approx. 1.91:1 ratio). Avoid very tall or narrow images.

4. Creating a Template

Fill in the Create New Template form. All required fields are marked below.

1

Select Business *

Choose which WhatsApp number this template belongs to (Garment Retail or LawAid AI).

2

Template Name *

Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only. This name is permanent — it cannot be changed after submission.

Examples: summer_sale_2026, appointment_reminder, order_shipped

3

Category *

  • 📣 Marketing — promotions, offers, newsletters
  • 🔧 Utility — order updates, appointment reminders, account alerts
  • 🔐 Authentication — OTPs and verification codes

Choosing the right category affects cost and deliverability. Utility templates have lower per-message cost than Marketing.

4

Language *

Choose the primary language. You can submit separate templates for Hindi (hi) and Marathi (mr) if needed.

5

Header (optional)

Choose from:

  • None — text body only
  • 📝 Text — a bold one-line title (max 60 chars). Use {{1}} for one variable.
  • 🖼️ Image — displays an image above the message body. Paste the URL from Assets Library or click 📁 Browse Assets.
  • 🎬 Video — embeds a video thumbnail. Paste a public MP4 URL.
  • 📄 Document — attaches a document. Paste a public PDF URL.
6

Body *

The main message text (max 1024 chars). Use numbered placeholders for personalisation:

  • {{1}} — first variable (e.g. customer name)
  • {{2}} — second variable (e.g. order number)
  • … and so on

When you add variables, Example value fields appear automatically. Fill these in — Meta uses them to review your template.

Example body:
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} has been dispatched and will arrive by {{3}}. Track it here: https://track.example.com

7

Footer (optional)

A short line of grey text shown below the body (max 60 chars). Commonly used for opt-out instructions.

Example: Reply STOP to unsubscribe

8

Buttons (optional — up to 3)

Click + Add Button and choose a type:

  • 💬 Quick Reply — customer taps to send a fixed reply (e.g. "Yes, interested" or "No thanks"). Enter just the button label.
  • 🔗 URL — opens a link when tapped. Enter label + full URL. Append {{1}} at the end of the URL for a dynamic suffix (e.g. https://yourstore.com/order/{{1}}).
  • 📞 Phone — dials a number when tapped. Enter label + full phone number in international format (e.g. +919876543210).
Don't mix Quick Reply buttons and CTA buttons (URL/Phone) in the same template.
9

Submit for Approval

Click 📤 Submit for Meta Approval. A success message will confirm the template ID and initial status (PENDING). Meta typically reviews templates within a few minutes to a few hours.

6. Template Approval & Notifications

Approval statuses

StatusMeaning
PENDINGSubmitted to Meta and awaiting review. Usually resolves within minutes to a few hours.
APPROVEDReady to use in Broadcasts and outbound messages.
REJECTEDMeta did not approve the template. The rejection reason is shown in the notification banner and in the template list.
PAUSEDTemplate has been paused by Meta, usually due to low customer engagement or quality issues. Cannot be sent until re-enabled.

How notifications work

When Meta approves or rejects a template, it sends a webhook event to your app's /api/webhook URL. The app automatically captures this and displays a notification banner at the top of the Templates tab the next time you open it.

  • ✅ Green banner = APPROVED — template is ready to use
  • ❌ Red banner = REJECTED — reason is shown in the banner

Dismiss each banner by clicking ✕. Dismissed notifications will not reappear.

Prerequisite: For notifications to work, your webhook URL (https://your-site.netlify.app/api/webhook) must be subscribed to the message_template_status_update field in Meta Business Manager → WhatsApp Manager → Configuration → Webhook fields.

Subscribing to template status webhook events

1

Open Meta Business Manager

Go to business.facebook.com → WhatsApp Manager → Configuration.

2

Edit Webhook Fields

Click Manage next to Webhook fields and enable message_template_status_update.

3

Save

Click Subscribe. Meta will now send status events to your webhook URL automatically.

Checking status manually

Open the Templates tab, select a business in the Your Templates card, and click Load. The current status from Meta is always shown live.

If your template is rejected

  • Read the rejection reason in the notification banner or template list card
  • Common reasons: promotional language in Utility templates, vague body text, missing opt-out in Marketing templates
  • Delete the rejected template and resubmit with a corrected version (use a new name or the same name — Meta allows resubmission)

7. Using Approved Templates in Broadcasts

Once a template is APPROVED, you can use it in the Broadcasts tab.

1

Go to 📢 Broadcasts

Click the Broadcasts tab in the top navigation.

2

Select Message Type → Template

Click the 📋 Template card in the Message Type row.

3

Enter the Template Name

Type the exact template name as submitted (e.g. summer_sale_2026). This must match exactly — case-sensitive.

4

Fill in variables and media

  • Add body variable values (e.g. {{1}} = {first_name} for personalisation)
  • If your template has an Image/Video/Document header, paste the media URL from Assets Library
  • Add button parameters if your template has dynamic URL or quick-reply buttons
5

Select recipients and send

Choose all opted-in contacts or filter by tag, then click 📤 Send Campaign.

Personalisation variables

In the variable value fields, you can use these tokens — they are replaced per contact at send time:

TokenReplaced with
{name}Contact's full name
{first_name}First word of contact's name
{phone}Contact's phone number

8. Tips & Best Practices

  • Use Utility category for transactional messages — order updates, appointment reminders, and delivery notifications are cheaper and faster to approve as Utility rather than Marketing.
  • Always include an opt-out in Marketing templates — add a footer like "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" or a Quick Reply button. Meta requires it and customers expect it.
  • Keep body text specific — vague bodies like "Check this out!" are often rejected. Be clear about what you are communicating.
  • Use meaningful example values — when your body has {{1}}, fill in a realistic example like "Priya Sharma" not "Example1". Better examples lead to faster approvals.
  • Image headers get more engagement — if you have a product image or banner, use it. Visuals significantly increase open and reply rates on WhatsApp.
  • One template per use case — don't try to make one template cover every situation with lots of variables. Targeted, specific templates perform better and are easier to approve.
  • Check quality rating — after sending, low quality scores (low opens, high blocks) can cause Meta to pause your template. Monitor customer feedback.

9. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
Template not appearing in the list Click Load in the Your Templates card. If still missing, check the business selector — templates are per WhatsApp number.
Submission fails with "Meta API error" Check that the template name uses only lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores. Also verify the business's access_token has whatsapp_business_management scope.
Template rejected — "Variable parameters are not allowed" Make sure your variables are numbered sequentially starting from 1: {{1}}, {{2}}, etc. Do not skip numbers.
No approval notification received Ensure message_template_status_update is subscribed in Meta Webhook fields. Also run supabase-migration-v6.sql to create the notifications table.
Image not showing in broadcast The image URL must be a publicly accessible HTTPS link. Test by opening the URL in an incognito browser tab. URLs from Supabase Assets Library are always public.
Delete button doesn't work Confirm you have whatsapp_business_management permission on the access token. Some templates may also have a deletion cooldown period after approval.
Asset upload fails Run supabase-migration-v6.sql in Supabase SQL Editor first to create the storage bucket. File must be under 10 MB.
