Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our WhatsApp AI Agent service ("Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with us via WhatsApp. This Service is operated by Neeraj Khattar ("we", "us", or "our") and powers customer communications for businesses including Garment Retail and LegalAid AI.

1. Information We Collect

When you send a message to one of our WhatsApp business numbers, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of messages you send to us
  • Timestamps of your messages
  • Message type (text, image, document)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Route your messages to the appropriate human agent when needed
  • Improve our AI responses and service quality
  • Maintain records of customer interactions for business purposes

3. AI-Powered Responses

Our Service uses artificial intelligence (powered by Anthropic's Claude) to automatically respond to common enquiries. Your messages may be processed by this AI system to generate responses. Complex or sensitive queries are routed to human agents for personalised assistance.

4. Data Storage

Your messages and contact information are stored securely in our database hosted on Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption and security practices. Data is stored on servers in the United States.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Meta Platforms: As required to operate the WhatsApp Business API
  • Anthropic: Message content may be processed by Claude AI to generate responses
  • Service providers: Who assist us in operating our platform under strict confidentiality agreements

6. Data Retention

We retain your message history for up to 12 months to provide continuity of service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of AI-powered responses (your messages will be handled by a human agent)

8. WhatsApp & Meta

Our Service operates through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.
